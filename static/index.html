VIRTUAL FITTING STUDIO · PROTOTYPE
YOUR FIT, IN THREE STEPS

Make yourself at home.

  1. Let the camera in. Click Start camera and allow access in your browser. Use good lighting and stand 1–2 meters away.
  2. Find your frame. Face forward with your shoulders and hips visible. Enter your height. For a better estimate, include your head and feet and stand upright.
  3. Try something on. Pick a color and size, then click Try on. Move naturally, switch colors, or remove the shirt without stopping the camera.

This is a 2D visual preview, not a simulation of fabric or garment fit. Sample garments and prices are for this prototype.

Camera processing stays in your browser. The pose engine downloads on first use, so an internet connection is needed.
